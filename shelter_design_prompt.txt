THERMOSHELTER SIH26051 — V1 SHELTER DESIGN SCHEMA

We are building the V1 internal-hackathon prototype for SIH26051:

"Software Based Model Development for Design of Area Specific Shelter for Thermal Comfort Maintenance."

The V1 concept is focused primarily on high-altitude cold regions, especially Ladakh/Leh, with Shimla as the secondary cold-region comparison. Karur and Jaipur remain hot-climate comparison cases.

The following datasets are FROZEN and must NOT be modified:

- data/raw/jaipur_weather_2026.csv
- data/raw/karur_weather_2026.csv
- data/raw/leh_weather_2026.csv
- data/raw/shimla_weather_2026.csv
- data/materials/material_properties.csv

TASK:

Design the V1 shelter-configuration schema that will later be used by the thermal simulation engine.

IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES:

1. Do not duplicate material properties inside the shelter schema.

Example:
wall_material_id = MAT-001

The actual thermal conductivity, density, etc. must come from:

data/materials/material_properties.csv

2. Shelter dimensions should be stored separately from material properties.

3. Derived values such as floor_area_m2, wall_area_m2 and roof_area_m2 should be clearly identified as calculated/derived fields if they are not manually entered.

4. Do not invent engineering values.

5. Keep the schema extensible for V2.

6. Use SI units consistently.

7. Use IDs to reference materials rather than copying material names and properties.

8. The schema must support comparison of multiple shelter designs under the same weather conditions.

9. The schema must support testing the same shelter design across Leh, Shimla, Karur and Jaipur.

10. The schema should eventually allow the thermal engine to calculate:

- heat conduction
- thermal mass effects
- solar gains
- ventilation-related heat loss
- approximate indoor temperature
- thermal comfort/risk

BUT DO NOT IMPLEMENT THESE CALCULATIONS YET.
